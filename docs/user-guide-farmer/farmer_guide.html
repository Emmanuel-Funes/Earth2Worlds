Farmer User Guide & Technical Field Manual

A comprehensive, visual walkthrough for agricultural producers and farm managers. Discover how to delimit your field anywhere in Argentina, cross-reference 34 years of NASA Earth science telemetry, and obtain a high-precision 3-year crop rotation plan optimized for soil health, water retention, and frost risk mitigation.

2. Step 1: Delimit your Field in Argentine Territory

Define your production boundaries using high-resolution Esri World Imagery with integrated Leaflet and Geoman vector drawing engines.

Figure 2: Satellite map interface with Leaflet-Geoman toolbars and Google Coordinates search bar. 02_step1_field_delimitation_map.png
📍
Google Coordinates Search
Paste coordinates directly copied from Google Maps (e.g. -33.6527, -65.4786) into the input box and click "Search Place" to automatically zoom and center the map on your exact property.
📐
Vector Drawing Tools
Use the left sidebar toolbar with unambiguous single-language labels: Rectangle, Polygon, Edit, Delete, and Undo to delineate irregular farm perimeters.
🇦🇷
Argentine Border Validation
An automated spatial boundary check validates that the polygon is located inside Argentina, preventing invalid global coordinates from contaminating local soil databases.
Figure 3: Delimited parcel in San Luis, Argentina. The surface area (61.46 ha) and centroid coordinates are computed instantly. 03_step1_parcel_drawn_calculated.png
💡
Real-time Geometric Calculation: As soon as you close your polygon, the system computes the exact geodesic surface area in hectares and the centroid coordinates [Lat, Lon]. Once the parcel is valid, the green button "Confirm Parcel and Continue →" unlocks in the bottom-right corner of the map.

4. 34 Years of NASA Data at your Service

Earth2Worlds accesses a 34-year historical archive from the NASA POWER agroclimatology project (1991 to 2025) directly over your parcel's centroid.

Figure 5: High-visibility 34-year NASA scientific banner positioned at the top of the agronomic evaluation area. 05_step3_telemetry_cards_and_banner.png

Satellite Constellation

5. The 6 Satellite Information Cards

RotaTerra organizes NASA Earth observation streams into 6 specialized satellite cards, complete with high-visibility interaction buttons.

HLS / MODIS 30 m / 250 m

🌱 Active Vegetation (NDVI)

Measures photosynthetically active green biomass, historical photosynthetic vigor curves, and parcel vigor index.

NASA SMAP 9 km EASE-Grid

💧 Sowing Moisture

Microwave radiometer data measuring root-zone and surface volumetric soil moisture (0–100 cm) in m³/m³.

GPM IMERG 10 km grid

🌧️ Accumulated Precipitation

Integrated multi-satellite precipitation records with explicit start and cut-off dates for the accumulation period.

VIIRS / POWER 1 km / 0.5°

❄️ Soil Temperature

Radiometric soil and canopy skin temperatures in °C. Critical for germinative thermal floors and late frost alarms.

ECOSTRESS / POWER 70 m / 0.5°

🌿 Soil Evaporation (ET)

Atmospheric evaporative demand and reference evapotranspiration ET⌟ expressed in mm/day.

NASADEM 30 m SRTM

⛰️ Topography & Slope

Elevation above sea level, percentage slope, and hydrological runoff azimuth to predict water ponding or erosion.

⚡
Background Download & Interactive Buttons: While the 5-year temporal series is downloading, each card displays a prominent status pill. Once the archive is ready, the pill transforms into an enlarged, clickable "📈 View Chart (5y)" button that opens the deep temporal analytics viewer.
Agronomic Planning

6. The 3-Year Crop Rotation Plan (INTA & FAO)

Beyond recommending a single upcoming crop, RotaTerra projects a sustainable, multi-year rotation sequence.

Figure 6: Triennial rotation cards (Year 1, Year 2, and Year 3) with Recommendation Indices and category summary cards. 06_triennial_crop_rotation_plan.png
🥇
Year 1: Upcoming Season
Selects the #1 ranked agronomic choice (e.g. Grain Sorghum with 87% Recommendation Index) based on immediate water availability and soil conditions.
🥈
Year 2: Subsequent Season
Enforces biological alternation after Year 1 (e.g. rotating from Sorghum to Sunflower) to respect INTA sanitary intervals and preserve protective ground cover.
🥉
Year 3: Third Season
Closes the triennial cycle (e.g. rotating to Early Soybean), alternating taproot and fibrous root systems while replenishing nitrogen via leguminous symbiosis.
🎯
Interactive Exploration: Clicking on Year 2 or Year 3 smoothly scrolls the view down to inspect how each candidate crop scored for that specific future campaign.
Deep Science

7. Comprehensive Agronomic Criteria: Your Field vs. Optimal

Transparent scientific justification. Every crop evaluation can be expanded to reveal up to 10 rigorous INTA/FAO physiological criteria.

Figure 7: Crop candidate cards classified as Recommended (green), With Caution (amber), or Not Recommended (red). 07_agronomic_recommendation_cards.png
Figure 8: Expanded Agronomic Criteria showing direct field observations compared against physiological thresholds. 08_agronomic_criteria_expanded.png
🌡️
Soil Temperature for Sowing
Compares 10/10 seasons of NASA thermal data against the minimum germination threshold (e.g. 15°C required vs 19°C observed).
⏳
Growing Degree-Days (Thermal Sum)
Validates whether thermal accumulation (°Cd) allows full physiological maturity before the historical first autumn frost.
💧
Cycle Water Demand
Calculates whether historical effective precipitation plus stored soil water covers at least 60% of crop evapotranspiration.
